    About this map

    The 1937 layer is the Zagazig, Egypt–Town Series 1:5,000 plan published by the Survey of Egypt (Maṣlaḥat al-Misāḥah wa-al-Manājim), scanned by the American Geographical Society Library, University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee, via Wikimedia Commons. Public domain.

    It is placed on the modern map using the scale printed on its own km grid, then checked against railways, canals and roads. Expect a few meters of difference in places; many canals have since been filled in and are now roads.
